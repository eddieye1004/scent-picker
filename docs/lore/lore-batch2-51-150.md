### 51. frederic-malle-lipstick-rose（排名 117）
- 名称：Frédéric Malle Lipstick Rose
- 背书类型：经典故事
- 文案：调香师 Ralf Schwieger 于 2000 年为 Frédéric Malle 创作，意在复刻老好莱坞女星唇膏的蜡质香气——玫瑰与紫罗兰交织。香评人记述其意象为「half Gilda, half Silvana Mangano」（一半吉尔达、一半西尔瓦娜·曼加诺）。
- 来源链接：https://www.fredericmalle.com/product/19566/50155/parfums/lipstick-rose/by-ralf-schwieger ；https://boisdejasmin.com/2005/08/frederic-malle-lipstick-rose-perfume-review.html ；https://perfumesociety.org/talking-scents-ralf-schwieger-creator-lipstick-rose-new-rosie-autograph/
- 备注：官网 fetch 403、Bois de Jasmin fetch 511，两处均仅搜索摘要佐证。官网摘要："Ralf Schwieger once took part in a perfume contest that Frédéric Malle had staged for young perfumers. Frédéric sampled their perfumes one by one, blindfolded, ..."；Bois de Jasmin 摘要："Ralf Schwieger's aim was to create a scent conjuring a woman who is half Gilda, half Silvana Mangano, in her film from 1951 'Anna'"。Perfume Society 专访已 fetch，确认 Schwieger 为创作者（但无创作细节）。2000 年为通行记载。品牌「盲品选才」故事亦见官网摘要，可作辅助叙事。
